あらすじ#

This Fandisc contains the following scenarios

・「Invisible Attacker」

One day, the boys at school act strangely and sexually assault the girls at school. The boys, who are clearly under some evil influence, even start to attack Tina and Setsu who can’t transform! What will Forneus do when the event has come out like this!?

・「Minotaur’s Bride」

Sacred Claire has lost and has been captured. Her life as a prisoner and being violated everyday by the sorcery beast, Minotaur, has begun. Rajiel’s cold smile appears as she looks down upon Claire’s humiliated and disgraceful state. What will be the fate of Sacred Claire who continues to suffer pregnancy, birthing, and being milked!?

・「Starting from today, I am the protagonist! Dream and ambitious of extravagant life」

Gotou, an executive of Foundation, is the embodiment of authority and lust. He takes the advantage when Forneus’s absence and succeeds in placing submission seals on Tina and Setsu. With the power of the seal, he takes away Tina’s and Setsu’s freedom. Without their freedom, the girls are violated At home, at school, and even after transformation. Gotou even goes as far as targeting the girl who noticed Tina’s and Setsu’s abnormalities. Too bad for the girls, his libido has no limit.

・「Fleeting Beauty of Devil’s Flower」

Noire fights hard in order to help Claire who is being captured and continued to be raped by Forneus. However, she suffers great defeats and her anal is used as compensation thoroughly each time. In the end, she is able to meet with Claire, but what awaits her is a harsh reality.

・「A Certain Executive’s Boredom」

Shax, an executive Gatea, came to visits Forneus in the human world, but he can’t find Forneus when he arrived. When Shax learns that Forneus is actually fighting Sacred Claire and Sacred Noir, he decides to attack the girls to lure Forneus out. So, Claire and Noire are violated as a signal for Forneus to present himself. Too bad for the girls, Forneus is still nowhere to be found. E
