あらすじ#

There once was an idol who took the world by storm—

A girl hailed as “the once-in-ten-thousand-years beauty.”

Her name was Mirei Kuramoto.

With a translucent, almost fairy-like appearance,

And a radiant innocence that seemed to heal all who saw her,

She was called the very definition of a true idol.

But as time passed and she grew closer to adulthood,

The gazes cast upon her began to change—

No longer of admiration, but of crude desire.

They were the kinds of stares no idol should ever acknowledge.

Just as she struggled to stay true to herself,

A man began to move behind the scenes.

His name was Fumito Ukai—

A producer at her talent agency.

A man who had brought countless idols and entertainers to success,

And crushed just as many underfoot in the process.

This is a story that unfolds away from the spotlight—

A tale of what happens offstage.
