thanklessly under the management of his boss, Ms Ayaka Takami, as a

designer of brassieres for women’s lingerie maker Dejour.

When he got the chance to design part of the season’s main

product line, it was offered with Takami’s usual coldness:

“You. Can you make a J-cup bra?”

It was a backhanded offer, as there was nobody who would buy such a size.

But with few prospects for advancement and nothing to lose, Ryouta went for it.

“I’ll do it!”

“Of course we can’t afford product testing. You’ll have a budget of about $100.”

“But–”

“This isn’t a hot seller. We can’t waste more than we expect to earn back.”

“But–”

As Ryouta’s spirits tumbled he was introduced to his design partner.

As soon as he saw her, they both let out a surprised gasp.

“Ah!”

“Ah!”

Before him stood his old senpai from college. Shiori Houjou. For the longest time he’d

had a one-sided crush on her. She went off and got married. Ryouta never

forgot her incredible breasts, regal and large. The biggest he’d ever seen.
