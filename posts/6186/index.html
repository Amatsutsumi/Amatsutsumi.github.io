あらすじ#

Suddenly, Konomi realizes that she is standing in an unfamiliar space.

As she wonders where she is, a beautiful woman appears before her.

“I am the goddess, and unfortunately you have died.”

However, the goddess tells her to go to the Spring of Time to prepare the holy water necessary to revive her.

In order to adventure in a harsh other world, Konomi is equipped with a strong body as a hero and tools filled with magical powers, and descends onto the land of the other world.

“But what am I supposed to do with no food or money!?”

How will the confused Konomi’s adventure turn out?

In a world where there is no demon king to defeat, what is the value of a hero?
