あらすじ#

You work as a private tutor.

I don’t know if it’s because there’s a shortage of staff or because of the good reputation, but this time I was assigned to be a private tutor for one child.

On a hot summer day, I headed to a room in an apartment and was greeted by a blonde girl named “Ayano”.

It might just be my imagination, but she seems a little suspicious in her behavior…

This is the beginning of my weekend life as a private tutor with Ayano-chan, a strong-willed girl. I will teach her while building trust through communication.

Even though she lightly brushes off pranks, it seems she does have some interest in matters of the opposite sex…?

If I can successfully help her pass the exam, she might show her gratitude in the way you desire.
