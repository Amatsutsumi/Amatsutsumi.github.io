あらすじ#

“Until this long holiday ends, I’ll take care of everything for you!”

A classmate suffers a severe injury, breaking her arms and legs…

And the protagonist ends up taking care of her.

Despite her straightforward and cool demeanor, she makes some embarrassing and outrageous requests,

which (using all of his self-control) he fulfills!

Becoming the hands and feet of his incapacitated classmate,

he ends up doing all sorts of things to her body…!
