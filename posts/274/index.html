あらすじ#

Protagonist Yosuke Ikeda was reminded of his own shortcomings in the daily presence of cool-headed, talented, universally popular lady Saya Mikogami.

She saw him being humiliated for his lewd tendencies and decided to fix him up by taking a private holiday to an uninhabited island.

At the same time as they trained Yosuke and Saya became lovers, as she showed him the ways of sexual discipline… a splendid baby-making life with the cool, perfect older girl!
