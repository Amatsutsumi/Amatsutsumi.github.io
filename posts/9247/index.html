あらすじ#

The protagonist, Daisuke Tomigo, acquired enormous funds through currency trading during his university days. Using this capital, he invested in real estate and stocks, becoming a wealthy man at a young age. Armed with his vast fortune, he obtained whatever he desired and indulged freely in women. Yet, finding nothing money couldn’t buy, he grew bored quickly.

Though his immense wealth had made life dull, a chance encounter sparked a new fascination—one that money alone couldn’t buy easily… He became intensely intrigued by women in love with other men, specifically married women. With no need to work and no time constraints due to his fortune, Daisuke built a condominium in the suburbs solely for this purpose. Living there as the owner, he began scouting for his target: a married woman.

Just as he was growing impatient, unable to find a suitable target, a couple moved into his hunting ground. Daisuke was intensely drawn to the stunningly beautiful, picture-perfect married woman, Kasumi Terauchi. He set her as his target, approaching her as both the building’s owner and a fellow resident. With smooth talk, he coaxed her into confiding her troubles. Learning she longed for a child but her husband refused to let her get pregnant, Daisuke timed his moves for the daytime hours when her husband was out working.

He succeeded in having a physical relationship with her, exploiting the gap in her heart that yearned for a child.
