Yet I did believe her!

Half the human population is in grave danger because of one magical girl.

An evil organization uses an inescapable weapon of mass slaughter, the Magical Nanomaterial Weapon (MNW).

It was magical girl Rina, a symbol of justice, who committed the greatest treason against mankind in all history. Because the girl had leaked the secret data to the evil organization, the dire massacre weapon was created! There is only one week until the enemies fully complete it! To prevent the use of the weapon, the protagonist must find the location of the enemy base from the traitor, magical girl Rina.

But the protagonist, having fallen in love with Rina, couldn’t believe that she had turned into a betrayer. He starts an investigation into whether it is true or false. He makes strenuous efforts to clear her name, yet only unfavorable evidences appears, proving the falsification of her records, past ties, and such.

He helplessly interrogates, persuades, and threatens her, yet Rina refuses to talk, only appealing that she does not know anything and wants him to believe her.
