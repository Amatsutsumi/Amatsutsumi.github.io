あらすじ#

One day, I casually opened the window.

One day, I casually opened the window and my eyes met those of “Moe Shibasaki,” who was peeking out of the window.

“Oh?　Moe?”

Oh, my brother. ……

Moei Shibasaki, who lives in the house next door, is a childhood friend.

The curtains on the window of Mouei’s room were drawn, and she was just sticking her head out.

But something was wrong and Moei looked flustered.

Just then, a strong wind blew.

The curtains were blown open, revealing the inside of Moei’s room.

Moei was completely naked and had a rope wrapped around her.

Moei’s face turned bright red, and in a panic, she closed the window and drew back the curtains.

I was left standing there in a daze.

Later that day, Moei called me to the park.

We sat on a bench, me and Moei, in silence.

Just when I was about to lose my patience with the silence, Moei finally opened her mouth.

And then ——
