あらすじ#

Hiromi, the protagonist, is being ostracized simply because his puberty is delayed. Worried about her son, his mother knocks on the door of a certain hospital, clinging to a sliver of hope.

What kind of hospital has he been brought to?

The name of the hospital is Daijuji Hospital, and the department is… the Virginity Department!? Is virginity something that can be treated!?

‘This requires hospitalization,’ he is told, the verdict delivered with ruthless detachment. And so begin the days of medical examinations.

Can virginity really be cured? And who will be the one to help him? His life as a hospital patient, filled with both anticipation and anxiety, is about to begin right here and now.
