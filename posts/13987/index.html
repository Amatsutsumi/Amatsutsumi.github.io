あらすじ#

I, Mizuhashi Ren, spent my school days working part-time jobs and living a gray youth with no romance whatsoever.

But that’s only a minor issue to me now.

That’s because I have two adorable and sexy little sisters!

My sister Chitose is a soft-spoken, carefree girl, and my sister Iori is a sports-loving girl with an 80% tsun and 20% dere personality.

The moment I get home and open the front door, my harem of little sister heaven begins.

“I’m home, big brother! Well… shall we go to bed right away?”

“Tonight too… I won’t let you sleep until I’ve satisfied you…♪”

What will my fate be, with my little sisters begging me for sex every day?
