あらすじ#

A certain boy transfers to a certain school.

He has caused problems at his previous school, bullying anyone he does not like since the first day, his followers gradually increasing.

One day, a friend nervously warns him.

“……A terrifying teacher is coming.”

“Do you think I’m afraid of that?”

Meanwhile, a female teacher enters the classroom.

――Kyoudou Maeko, arrives at the school.

“I’ll teach you, how weak of a human being you are.”
