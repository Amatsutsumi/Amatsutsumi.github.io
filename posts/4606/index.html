あらすじ#

I was surrounded by beauty: over here my kawaii friend, over there my beautiful senpai…… Not that it mattered, romantically. Nobody gave an unpopular guy like me any attention.But my forever alone days were forever over.

My smartphone chirped an app alert. It was a government license and an ultimatum:

“You have been selected from national lottery for obligatory sexual partnership. Tap here to select a partner.”

Was this app about to MAKE me get laid!?

“I’mma have sex beyond my wildest dreams!!”

I thought as I pulled up what I hoped would be a list of hotties…but instead the app said,

“There are no eligible partners for your inferior genes.”

This app was crueler than the real world.

But then the app bugged out. It said,

“Partner registration: How many partners (targets) do you want?”

and for some reason I was able to define the settings.

I was allowed to actually input names.

So began my glitch-abusing sexfest with beautiful ladies……
