あらすじ#

The hot days of summer have arrived… and I have returned to my rural hometown.

Gramps came to pick me up in his car and we head back to his house.

There, there was a girl in a one-piece dress……

Catching bugs, playing tag, flicking skirts, the summer festival,

a girl drying her clothes next to the river…

these sweet and sour summer memories, turn me from a boy, to a man.
