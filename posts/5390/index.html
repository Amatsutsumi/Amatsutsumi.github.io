あらすじ#

The protagonist Junjiro was pessimistic and unhappy as a child because he was born with a weak body, but gradually regained happiness due to his best friend Yousuke who was always by his side and supporting him.

Shiori, who met them during school was also saved by Yousuke.

The three became good friends, but eventually Junjiro fell in love with Shiori. However Junjiro also noticed that Yousuke had feelings for Shiori too, and unable to control his own feelings, Junjiro told Yousuke how he felt and got him to help confess his love for Shiori and started dating.

As time passed, Junjiro and Shiori married and moved to the city. Junjiro eventually became concerned that he couldn’t satisfy Shiori at night due to his physical weakness.

One day, Yousuke came to their city for work, and Junjiro who wanted to pay Yousuke back for his help in the past, forced him to stay with them rather than rent an appartment.

Afterwords, when Junjiro returned home from a business trip, the usually passive Shiori, shown him sexual skills that he had never seen before. Worried about why his wife suddenly changed after being left with their friend, on his next business trip, he set up hidden cameras in the house. However, nothing was shown on them and he became deeply ashamed that he suspected his wife and best friend.

But from there her mood and behavior seemed to change. Is it his imagination, or…
