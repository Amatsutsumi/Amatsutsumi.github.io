あらすじ#

Will he become the saviour of the women in the prison?

Or will he become a merciless surveillant?

The setting is in the midst of a great war in the future of the 21st century.

The world has spiraled in a whirlpool of chaos and people struggle to survive in this world of darkness.

The protagonist is called by instruction of the army from his rural hometown to an army Asylum for females as a guard.
