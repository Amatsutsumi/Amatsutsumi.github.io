Then, his father goes away on a business trip for a few weeks for work. That night, the mother sneaks into the protagonist’s room. Unbeknownst to the father himself, a serious illness a few years ago has reduced his sperm count to the point where he is unable to conceive naturally, so he wants his son, the protagonist, to fulfill that duty.
