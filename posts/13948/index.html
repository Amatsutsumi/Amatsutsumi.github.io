A world under control of the evil Empress. You vow to return freedom to the people by conquering the conquerors… but you are a mere and powerless human.

The world remains under their control.

In your quest have learned about a human colony where monster girls “do various things” (wink) to the residents.

With the (ecchi) adventure behind you, days of lust follow…
