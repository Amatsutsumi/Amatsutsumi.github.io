别名#

あらすじ#

A fan disc of sorts for various Leaf games.

Includes:

  • Witsarunemitea Senki: Action game featuring characters from Utawarerumono

  • Leapon: Ponjan game where you can choose from six opponents: Eruruu, Aruruu, Takase Mizuki, Mikage Subaru, Kurihara Touko, and Kida Emiri

  • Guendina no Majo: Real-time strategy game that takes place after Natsumi’s route in Magical Antique

  • Routes Extra Mission: Two short scenarios that take place after the main story of Routes

  • Leaf no Tou (Leaf Battle Characters): 50 stage RPG with real-time elements, with playable characters from Magical Antique, Tasogare, Utawarerumono, and Routes

  • And other omake material
