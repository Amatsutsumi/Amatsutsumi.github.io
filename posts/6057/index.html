あらすじ#

The recently-founded Rokkasei Gakuen doesn’t have a rich history. One of its notable features is that students will select their specialization during the second term of their first year. Haruma decided to pursue the confectionery course that had been added this school year for the simple reason that he loves sweet a lot.

Nonoka, who loves both eating and making sweets

Yuzuki, who looks to be perfect at everything, but is actually quite clumsy

Fuuka, who possess maternal instincts and fortitude behind her quiet demeanour

Miel, who has a discerning palate, despite her lack of motivation and energy

Haruma’s meetings with these unique girls in his class draws him further not only into the charm of sweets, but also sweet sweet love…

[From Hau~ Omochikaeri!]
