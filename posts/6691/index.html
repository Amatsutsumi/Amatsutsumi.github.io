あらすじ#

Koji is just a university student. But one day, an angel, Chamu, suddenly appears in his room. According to her, there is an egg of God in his testicles. But how can you take it out? Chamu smiles and tries to cut his testicles with a sickle. Setsuna, who lives below his room, comes to his room in anger, “What the hell are you doing!?” Koji and Chamu explain what’s happened to them. Setsuna listens to it and says, “You just ejaculate!” Koji tries to run away, but a poisonous tail of Setsuna strikes his neck…. As a matter of fact, Setsuna is a devil. “I’ve finally found where the egg is!” Setsuna takes off his clothes…. Chamu soon cuts in because she doesn’t want to lose the egg. Like this, an epic battle for the egg starts….
