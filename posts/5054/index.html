あらすじ#

“I wish to destroy the world.”

“Huh? You’re asking why I want to destroy the world?”

I was born into a prestigious conglomerate family—blessed with wealth, basking in fame, brilliant-minded, strikingly handsome, and not just a refined gentleman but also highly skilled in martial arts. A man of both wisdom and courage.

Not only was I gifted by nature, but I also grew to be broad-minded and magnanimous—truly a perfect being.

Everyone, myself included, acknowledges me as a “chosen one of God.”

…And that—being chosen by God—is what pisses me off.

“Does it sound strange to say that’s why I want to destroy the world?”

This world, created by God—I, the one chosen by God, will destroy it.

Rebellion! Treason! Chaos! Let us revel in unrest.

At the end of this destruction, I’ll show you the new world I’ve created.

Now then—let us heap on the sins, and face divine judgment!!
