あらすじ#

Unhappy middle aged salaryman Taro Onihei (age 40).

Even the new recruits instinctively disliked him.

Only one person in the company treated him nicely,

and that was beautiful Kaori, the same tender woman

who was the object of every employee’s affection.

When it turned out Kaori was getting married, it

crushed Onihei’s tiny sliver of hope.

At this time, poor crushed Onihei was given a necktie.

Who was the gift-giver? A stranger. Was the tie some symbol

of his strangled dream of love? No… it was a talisman

capable of immense hypnotic power. Anyone

who laid eyes on the tie would fall under a spell.

Absurd.

But Onihei wore the tie. And his life changed.

  • Harem hypnosis

  • Multiple endings

  • 9 characters (incl. all men and women)
