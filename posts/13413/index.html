Onsen Okami Yogari Naki ~Yukiguni no Miboujin Oyako~

2007年5月11日 · 1 分

ゲーム情報#

  • ブランド: Kuro Hina
  • 発売日: 2007-05-11
  • スコア: 6.29/10 (10 人評価)
  • 日本語名: 温泉女将よがり鳴き～雪国の未亡人母娘～
  • 英語名: Onsen Okami Yogari Naki Yukiguni no Miboujin Oyako

あらすじ#

In a country where snow is ubiquitous, there exists a small ryokan called [Shizu] that is run by a landlady and a female cook, her daughter.

In order to increase the attractiveness of the place that is positively impacted due to a ski resort being located in close proximity, the local tourism association group considers to erect a hotel there. However, the landlady vehemently denies to offer to sell her property for this purpose.

Here the impatient tourism association decides on a man for the job, a professional “breaker” named Musashi Ryuuma who doesn’t shy away from both legal and illegal methods to fulfill his goals.

Soon, Ryuuma is booking a lodging in [Shizu].

According to the information he gathered beforehand there are only two people living in the ryokan at the time: Landlady Shiratori Chizuru and her daughter Shiratori Miyuki.

Ryuuma contemplates whether he should take advantage of this weakness.

There must be a way to break them, both if need be.

And so the curtain rises for Ryuuma’s mission to “break” them both.
