あらすじ#

The protagonist is a virgin who works as a live-in janitor at a prestigious academy for young ladies. One day, by a sudden stroke of luck, he gains the ability to use hypnosis. He uses this power to make the girls at the academy mistakenly perceive him as a beautiful transfer student. With this hypnotic influence, he engages in various activities with the girls, such as joint bathroom breaks, love discussions, practicing kisses, and even comparing underwear. And, of course, the possibilities go far beyond that. He takes advantage of his position as a girl among girls to indulge in every possible pleasure. Interestingly, thanks to the hypnotic power, everything he does is met with gratitude from the violated young ladies. This marks the beginning of the protagonist’s second erotic life as a beautiful transfer student.
