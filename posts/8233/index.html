あらすじ#

“Aaah … Father-in-law! It feels good … It feels so good!”

An incredible sight was spreading in front of me.

When I got home earlier than usual, I witnessed my wife having sex with a man … and the partner was my father.

My vision went pitch black and when I noticed it, I was lying on the sofa in the living room.

“Oh, thank God. It looks like you woke up. I was worried.”

“… Oh. Mother-in-law …”

It was mother-in-law, Natsukawa Midori, who is my father’s remarried partner from two years ago, called out to me.

“Oh, mother-in-law … The truth is ――”

―― I gave up and confessed everything.

Midori kept her calm and listened to me from beginning to end.

When I finished talking, she nodded a little, convincingly.

“Honestly … I thought that was the case after all. Daijiro-san’s appearance has been strange recently …

Thank you for speaking honestly. I’m more worried about you.

It would have been quite a shock to witness the moment of affair … ?”

“Yes … That is … Already …”

“… I understand. Just leave this to me …”

Midori said that, she gently leaned her body next to me and

“Hey … I think we should act together. With stronger connection than ever … Yes, as partners who vow to revenge.”
