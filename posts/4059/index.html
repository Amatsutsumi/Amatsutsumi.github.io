あらすじ#

  • His beloved wife, his daughter, fell victim to the ritual fornication of this depraved village. *

In the village of a remote island—

The only doctor of the local health clinic resigned.

His successor was chosen by the mainland committee of health practitioners: Ryoma Sankaku.

Ryoma uprooted his family and relocated to this remote place,

where there were no phone lines and no Internet. It was inconvenient, but

he was encouraged by the welcome reception of the villagers.

However, there was something the Sankaku family soon learned—

“Women of our village are wives to the men, all men of the village are their husbands.”

Life was a constant depraved ritual.

Men and women fornicated day and night.

Ryoma’s family was not exempt from this.

The village observed Five Rituals:

  1. Women of mental and physical ripeness will lay with the men

  2. Men of mental and physical ripeness will lay with the women

  3. The first Two Rituals shall be performed en masse.

  4. Injury between companions is forbidden.

  5. Refusing companions is forbidden.

A strange banquet of perversion closed off from the rest of the world. Happiness will crumble—

A gangbang ceremony without end, without choice, where all decency is surrendered.

For fans of humiliation, shame, and diverse violation fantasies.
