あらすじ#

“I want to be a little devil girl!”

My little sister, Ohana, came all the way to my room late at night to make a stupid declaration.

The stupid-looking magazine Ohana brought with her contained an equally stupid article.

“Today’s cute and popular girls are little devils☆”

“Let’s study all sorts of naughty things and get ready to make our yet-to-be-met prince fall in love with us☆”

Apparently, the purpose of this section was to teach proper sexual knowledge.

However, Ohana, being a simpleton, seems to believe the provocative words.

This is going to be interesting… right?

“Gufufu, I have no choice… In that case, I’ll be your naughty training partner.”

“Seriously? Are you sure?”

“…Huh? S-sure.”

“Sniff, sniff, I’m going to have some naughty fun with Onii-chan☆”

Although he’s a little intimidated by Ohana’s unexpectedly enthusiastic attitude, he pulls himself together and grins to himself.

It was all agreed upon, so let’s have as much fun as we want…!
