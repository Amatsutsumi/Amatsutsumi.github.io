あらすじ#

This here is the free nation of Liberalciora.

It’s a story about one boy and three girls, livin’ in the mansion of a certain well-off family.

Our protagonist, Fay Fleur Lenisphilia, lost his folks when he was just a kid, leaving him all alone in the world.

The ones who took him in as new family were the Lenisphilias next door. He’d known ’em since they were kids, and they grew up pretty much like siblings.

Luna Fleur Lenisphilia.

Melty Fleur Lenisphilia.

Tina Fleur Lenisphilia.

Thanks to the kindness of these three, who were there for him through his sadness, our guy slowly started to smile again.

And so, a few years after the tragedy…

The Lenisphilias were pretty much living like a real family by then, but

our hero had this one big ambition brewing in his heart.

He really wanted to pay back the huge kindness he’d gotten from his adoptive dad and mom in the Lenisphilia family, and from his adoptive big and little sisters…

Right around then, it was decided that the old head butler, who’d been running the household for ages, was gonna retire (hit the age limit, ya know),

and that’s when our hero’s long-held dream finally started to take shape.

“Please, hire me as the next butler!”

So, you’ve got this rookie butler, all fired up ‘cause he finally got his chance to pay ’em back, and

then there are the sisters, who just get all giddy and their hearts flutter watching him try so darn hard.

A service-themed rom-com where earnest feelings get all tangled up, kicks off right here!
