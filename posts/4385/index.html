あらすじ#

They’re beautiful. Those 5 pretty soldier heroines who save our world from looming darkness!

Yet, so few people know about those who supprot their existence …

Rare fans of these unnoticeable girls create blogs and try to promote these less-represented heroines’ daily lives and share their interests…

But the world is unsympathetic. “Other magical girls? That’s a load of fantasy crap! Grow up!”

One day an email arrives from an untraceable source.

“…Will you hear my story?”
