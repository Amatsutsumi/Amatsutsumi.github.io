あらすじ#

What do you think happens when a beautiful and innocent female teacher is stripped naked and exposed to her students? How does that make you feel?

She is stripped naked in the hallway by her students, she is spanked with force and made to dance naked in the clasroom… If she refuses she’s subjected to painful and humiliating punishments.

Even on her birthday, she was not allowed to blow out the candles on the cake normally, she was forced to do it with her farts.

And on my birthday, which is the most important day of anyone’s life, I had a day of shame that I will never forget.

This story is mainly about shame and humiliation, so there are no descriptions of sexual acts.
