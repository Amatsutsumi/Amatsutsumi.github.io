あらすじ#

It’s the end of the year. It’s been two years since I last visited my great-grandmother.

Since it’s been so long, I decided to take a bath with her, but…

(Here we go again…)

This is what’s been bothering me.

Whenever I see a naked woman, or when a woman clings to me,

that embarrassing thing between my legs gets bigger.

“Kaoru, come in. I’ll wash your back.”

“Huh…? N-no, it’s okay… I can do it by myself…”

That’s the official reason. The real reason is my crotch.

For some reason, I’m embarrassed about the growing thing between my legs.

I feel like I shouldn’t let great-grandmother see this.

…But I can’t hide it, and she finds out.

When my great-grandmother saw it, her elegant beauty beamed with a smile.

“It’s painful to leave it like that, right? Great-grandmother will heal you.”

And then, she placed her hand on my crotch…
