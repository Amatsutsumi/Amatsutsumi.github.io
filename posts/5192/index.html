あらすじ#

The protagonist, Mamori Morishima, is a hentai manga artist.

Unable to obtain his family’s acknowledgement of his work, he was disowned. Due to a referral to an editing job, he was assigned as a live-in dorm manager to Hinata Sou, a worn-down apartment built over 50 years ago.

Rent is free, but to remain he has to work for it. That’s when a dilemma was suddenly dropped upon him. The reality is that a pair of poor sisters, which are current residents of the dorm, don’t seem like they can pay for the rent

「Um… If it’s all right with you, I could pay with my body……」

Even though there isn’t any urgency, the unemployed older sister now backed to a cornered came to me with such a proposition. Being led by the flow of events unable to resist her big breasts in front of me, he moved his hands forward.

Even if we reached an agreement, in reality it is still an act of prostitution.

The little sister that eventually found out what was going on also made the decision to offer her body to lighten her older sister’s burden――

Thus begins the everyday life with a harem!

They also don’t seem to really hate it, so let’s have sex as much as possible!

But is this only a give-and-take relationship…?

Even if love is budding, will they forgive me?
