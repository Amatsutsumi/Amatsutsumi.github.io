あらすじ#

The past never fades—

the present stays unchanged—

the future won’t wait—

This is a story about ’the station.'

Once a relay point connecting stars, it’s now a massive hub on Earth’s surface for people who’ve forgotten its original purpose.

Still, generations change. One step at a time, ’the station’ gets its original function back, and people start rediscovering paths to the world of stars.

Communications with orbiting ‘stations’ that were cut off for literally generations are gradually getting online, giving interplanetary travel a new, albeit modest, beginning—an age of exploration is bound to come anew.

Yet, ’the station’ holds many secrets still, constantly opening new doors to unknown for its unsuspecting visitors and inhabitants both…

In the meantime, circus charges through the station full throttle.

Oh yes, a non-stop spectacle has begun!

Abandon all hope, those who would want to stop our troupe. Don’t even try.

Better meet the main stars of the show: a young ringmaster and a freak show girl.

Their stage may be no larger than a small plate, and yet, as I said, there is no stopping the two. Even they themselves cannot stop their rampage.

They’re off the rails, pulling everyone in their path in their orbit or/and mowing down everything on the way.

What’s that? As they run about as they please around the station, they end up getting way too many people involved, you say?

Where’s the circus headed? What will become of the pair?

Nobody knows. Yet. So stick around till the very end to see for yourself!
