あらすじ#

The protagonist tired from his work goes to a hot spring city to relax and found a “Regional horny tickets” at the information office there.

“Man, this is the best!”

This tickets can be used within 3 days in this city. The guide there recommended that he purchase them, so he spent 50,000 yen and bought 11 eleven. He used one and now he has 10 left to use within these 3 days.

“As long as I have this, I can really enjoy myself here!”

Where should he go to get rid of his stress? That’s up to you! They even say that if you use up your pass, you get a special reward…
