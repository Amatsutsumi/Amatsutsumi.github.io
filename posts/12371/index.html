あらすじ#

There was once a island nation called Jipang.

Here, young girls upheld a tradition called Ashikoki.

A school was established some 20 years ago to teach this custom to the young, and the best of these girls would perform at a major national tournament.

It was always Sakura’s dream to become an Ashikoki Shojo, or Footjob Girl.

But, without a trainer she couldn’t practice, nor could she appear in the tournament……

Sakura was sad, but fortune smiled on her when she met a man of dark hair and certain talents in an alley near the train station.

Yes, he was an Ashikoki Coach.
