あらすじ#

There are two schools adjoining each other. They are big rivals and often have a fight. In the schools, there are four girls admired by other students. Though they have special ability, they are a little bit peculiar. The main character is Yuki. He has an old friend, Nanatsu, who is one of the admired four girls. “I’ll become prime minister in the near future!” “Oh, you’re great.” He gets involved in various troubles, but he enjoys his school life with his positive personality…
