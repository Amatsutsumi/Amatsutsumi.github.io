あらすじ#

A nostalgic old letter took me back to my hometown.

The words in the letter spoke of how I was missed,

and how terribly lonely it was since I moved away.

The writing was feminine and curved.

It was from junior high school.

Summer is coming again, as it did many years ago,

and with the tender letter in my pocket,

fate prepares to take me on a new journey.

“I want to be here until summer’s end. With you…”

Reunite and live with Honoka, your hometown play mate from childhood.

The town is conservative and drab with nothing to do…

Fill the time by satisfying each other’s hungry bodies…
