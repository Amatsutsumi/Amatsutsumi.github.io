あらすじ#

“I can’t believe this, it’s just too easy, it’s hilarious!”

In the vibrant entertainment district, a girl approached me.

My heart raced with excitement, and… that was my mistake.

Before I knew it, I was tied up, mocked, and then…

“Oh. You’ve got quite a bit of cash, huh?”

As she pulled money from my wallet…

(I won’t let you go. I can’t just let you send me home like this!)

I struggled with a strength I had never summoned in my life before.

Then, the rope around my wrists loosened and came undone.

I seized her arm as if to rip it off and brought her down.

“Wha—What are you doing, you creep! Let go, you worthless piece of trash!”

“I told you to shut up. Ah, maybe if I do this, you won’t be able to talk back anymore?”

I covered her mouth with my own, and after a while… things changed.

“Hey, can you… untie my feet?”

“…Okay…”

The way she obediently agreed was eerily unsettling, and she began to loosen the ropes with a methodical touch.

What’s going on…? She’s acting like a completely different person than just a moment ago. What the hell happened?

This obedience… it’s as if she’s under some sort of hypnosis—.

That’s when I realized…
