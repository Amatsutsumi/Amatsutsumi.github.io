あらすじ#

In a world ruled by swords and magic, the only way for the poor to gain wealth is to enter the central arena, bet on themselves, and win.

But for the losers, what awaits is the debt in the sum they bet on themselves before the match.

And so, today, two female warriors arrive at the arena manager’s office.

One is Sera, a knight who lost in war.

The other is Rutejia, an elf sold off by slave traders.

Both, confident in their victory, look down on the manager with arrogance.

But inside, he’s smirking at their familiar attitude, already plotting how to profit from them… and how to have his fun.

Slowly, slowly, he’ll crush their pride—and show them the value of their bodies, of course, through pleasure!
