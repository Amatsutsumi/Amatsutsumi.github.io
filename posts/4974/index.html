あらすじ#

In the town of Najimi, there exists the “Six Wonders.”

Mysterious ghost stories passed around by children, urban legends. A commonplace occurence among children playing foolishly as they grow up. Once they grow up, they forget these trivial little stories.

The Six Wonders of Najimi.

“But…there’s actually one more.”

“Uh-huh, a seventh story.”

“You can never tell it to anyone.”

“They’re words you just can’t say.”

The seventh “wonder” is only spoken of in whispers, as a frightening rumor.

As sunset approaches, a cold and gloomy shadow looms over the town…
