Nantai Lesson ~Iinari Kanojo to Himitsu no Houkago~

  • スコア: 6.2/10 (13 人評価)

  • 英語名: Nantai Lesson Iinari Kanojo to Himitsu no Houkago

あらすじ#

“Ah… Youyama Honami-san, you’re wonderful as ever…”

Her name is Youyama Honami-san.

She’s a girl from my class, a member of the new Gymnast Club, good at both sports and studies, and well-mannered as well. She’s like an idol for our class. And she’s also… my crush.

I wish a miracle suddenly occurred, making her fall in love with me…

While thinking about that, I went home from school and picked up something resembling a fat pen from the roadside.

Lo and behold, “hypnotic item” was written on it. But even though I tested it on various people, no one seemed to be affected by hypnosis. Well, of course there’s no item that you can casually cast hypnosis with… That’s what I thought at least, but when I somehow managed to be all alone with whom I worship, I mentioned this hypnotic item to her, and it apparently picked her interest and she blurted out something unexpected.

“Wh-Why don’t you test it on me? Now that you mention it, I’m a bit intrigued.”

When Youyama-san said that, I pushed the button of the “hypnotic item.”
