あらすじ#

An alternate retelling of Zenra Toukou Shuukan Chijoku no Sanshuukan. After quitting her idol group Tears Haruka finds herself in a difficult situation. For breaking her contract she now owes a lot of money to her agency. Additionally her school is demanding she pay back the 3 years she didn’t commit to doing Total Nude Scholastic’s Week. Haruka is desperate, she cannot commit to returning to being an idol with tests coming up. She also refuses to do Total Nude Scholastic’s Week. Searching for advice she goes to her friend and fellow idol Yui.

Yui advises her to take odd jobs such as commercials and modeling work. That way she can both pay back her agency and avoid having to do Total Nude Scholastic’s Week. So Haruka announces to the media that although she is not returning to her idol group Tears she will continue her work as a public figure. She doesn’t know what’s in store for her however as the jobs which are available to her are increasingly humiliating.
