At this rate, he was bound to become a job-less shut-in after graduation.

However, she does not get the reception she expected, and he remains unchanged.

Ever determined, she decides to give his mind a bigger shock by getting taken by another man, right in front of his eyes.

Surely, that will straighten him out…
