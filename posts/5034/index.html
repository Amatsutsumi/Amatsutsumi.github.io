あらすじ#

“……Making a face like that, it’s not fair.”

At a dating agency, I reunited with Kei. I was a client who she was in charge of. A ring glinted on the left hand of my childhood friend.

After talking about each other’s life up to now at an izakaya, I took her to her home while she was drunk.

A splendid house, photos of a married couple, fertility drugs. My heart ached seeing the reality before me.

“It’s time for me to――”

When I was about to leave, she grabbed my arm――

Only for a short while, but it’s fine. Even if this becomes the worst act, it does not matter.

……With the first person whom I fell in love with, if this can make us fall in love again.
