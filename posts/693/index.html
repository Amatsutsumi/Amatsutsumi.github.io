あらすじ#

It’s been several years since social media platforms came to be, giving individuals the ability to carve out their own “spaces” online.

Now, SNS has become something so commonplace that it’s no longer particularly remarkable.

Yet, its popularity remains steady.

Why is that…?

Rumors claim that SNS has a “hidden” side—a darker, less visible face.

That in this “hidden world,” there exists a “space” where people lay bare their desires and seek to fulfill them.

It’s because there are those who are searching for a place to belong.

Here, there is a certain social network.

It’s not massive in scale, but it has a stable and dedicated following.

On it there exists a hidden entrance—a gateway to a world where people expose their deepest desires.

And there, you’ll find women who have devoted themselves to those desires…
