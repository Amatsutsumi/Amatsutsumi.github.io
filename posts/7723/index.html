あらすじ#

– On a holiday granted by god –

“Yuutarou-san! Yuutarou-san!”

As he comes to his senses, Hatsune is above him - she looks worried.

H: “I’m so glad! You woke up!”

“This is…”, Yuutarou stammers. What he sees: blue sky and white clouds. White waves are crashing into the beach.

Y: “I must be on some island.”

He tries to recall how he ended up here. He’s certain, he traveled to a certain country in South Asia. After settling a diplomatic issue with said country, he had gotten drunk on the ship heading home and felt the night breeze hitting his skin …

Y: “I fell off the ship?”

H: “I jumped immediately after you fell, but the ship didn’t notice us going overboard and just tuckered on. We swam and got washed up on this island, it seems.

Y: “Hatsune, did you carry me while swimming?”

H: “Yes, I’m a fantastic swimmer and I’m educated in saving people from drowning.”

Y: “Ah, I see. But there’s also sharks around here and there was a high chance that we both could have drowned before reaching this island. We’re pretty lucky to be still alive.” (Could it be that Hatsune was his good luck charm?)

His watch shows 8 am…

Y: “I’m sure everyone’s noticed our absence and they probably deployed a search party by now.

H: “I think so.”

They calmed down, but still hungry.

If only they had something to eat now…
