あらすじ#

When the village lost its crops and the corrupt governor showed no mercy, someone had to take a stand. That someone was a lone girl named Konagi.

Thanks to a mountain wizard she had the Power of Uprising, which could topple the region’s daimyo (feudal lord) with one blow.

Between Konagi and victory are the governor and his mysterious drug, a ninja-stealth shogunate lackey and a girl with the power of witchcraft.

What’s more, there are ghouls, tengu and kappa, and even legendary Edo celebrities Tadakatsu Honda, Shingen Takada and Gennai Hiraga… all wielding swords and dicks with supernatural powers!

Everyone and everything is beyond human. Just what is happening to this land!?

Taking down the daimyo alone won’t end this madness!

Konagi, your one-woman Uprising has begun!
