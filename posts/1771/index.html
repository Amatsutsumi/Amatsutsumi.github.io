Copulation to Completion!

Once sexual intercourse has begun, it must continue until all parties are satisfied. Sex Officers working for the Office of Sexual Affairs live by these words.

Their services are an outlet for sexual desire, an outlet which recharges mind and body and prevents the build up of SEXUAL FRUSTRATION. The one absolute requirement for becoming a Sex Officer is that you must be a PERVERT

This story is a documentary focused on the office’s community division, the department which deals the most with the public. Taking the viewpoint of a newly appointed Sex Officer, this is an exploration of an officer’s HONORABLE daily efforts to bring sexual relief to the public.
