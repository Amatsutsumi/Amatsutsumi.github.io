あらすじ#

All good things must come to an end, even the widely beloved fan-favorite critical-darling smash hit (citation needed) series Yarasete Teacher!

That’s right, it’s time for us to graduate from Kiboh Academy!

Looking back, it all began when Riko came to Kiboh Academy as a trainee.

She was a naive little thing who’d been sheltered by her overprotective family, and bad boy Yuta couldn’t resist playing all kinds of naughty pranks on her…

All Riko could do was take it in stride, despite knowing exactly what he was up to.

Of course, her demure reactions only fanned the flames of Yuta’s fuckboy spirit!

And since Yarasete Teacher! first started with Riko and Yuta,

I would like to close out this series with this cute couple, too.

So, on that note, “Graduation” will be all about depicting in glorious detail the

hot-and-heavy sex life of these two lovebirds, now that they’ve shacked up together!

We’ve also paid tribute to some fan favorites from previous installments.

I hope you’re looking forward to revisiting those classic scenes with us!

This work marks the end of the Yarasete Teacher! series, so don’t miss out!

[From Johren]
