あらすじ#

Stepsisters Asuka and Miki have been together with protagonist since childhood.

The protagonist swore to cherish them as a child, and the sisters fell in love with him, a love that quickly grew into something extreme.

One day, the protagonist received a love letter from a girl, and the sisters gone out of control!

They obstructed the protagonist, stating that they will never forgive any woman getting close to him.

The sisters then conveyed their sincere feelings to the protagonist, but…

Even if they’re sisters, women are enemies! I’m the one who will bear your child, my darling! The yandere sisters’ battle to be impregnated by the protagonist heats up!
