あらすじ#

“Kaoru, why don’t you come to our tea party?”

Kaoru Sekijima, decided to attend her neighbor’s tea party today in place of his mom, who had to leave for work on short notice.

“It’s a bit of a mess, but come on in,” said Suzuno Miyamori, a married woman who lives next door to us. She has a calm and gentle personality and gets along well with her mom. She was a beautiful, elegant woman, and I had a bit of a crush on her.

The other member is Saeko Kirishima. If Suzuno is a healing beauty, she is an intelligent cool-headed beauty. She is full of sensuality and motherly beauty.

Although I was unexpectedly nervous in front of two older beauties of different types, I enjoyed talking with the two beautiful ladies very much.

At first, the topic of conversation was quite normal, but gradually, the conversation started to get a bit awkward…

“Hmmm, so you’ve never been with a girl?”

caught off guard by the question, I made eye contact with these bewitching married beauties.
