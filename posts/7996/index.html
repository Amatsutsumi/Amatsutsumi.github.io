あらすじ#

“Money for men and sex for women. This is my Justice and Providence. It is also for convenience.”

The protagonist of this story is the manager of a convenience store. ​Lately he is vexed by the rapid increase in shoplifting incidents. At this point, an area manager is dispatched and proclaims, “HQ Sales is considering a review of your Franchising Contract.”

Why is the store management at fault rather than the offenders?

“Are you familiar with Kleptomania?”

Emotional stimulus from the act of shoplifting and the release of pleasurable feelings after success.

A recognised psychological disorder, hence these victims are to be protected.

“If the law protects the offenders rather than the victims…”

The manager hardens his resolve. If the crimes of the offenders are pardoned, then I will deliver retribution by my own hand.

“Kleptovaginas must be judged.”

“Gather the evidence, dispel all emotions and deliver my iron hammer of justice.”

“This is my Justice and Providence.”

The manager has his sights on three women suspected of shoplifting.

Underground idol: Zenmyoutsutsumi Mei

Tea serving office lady: Hanakura Yoshimi

Ex-hoodlum claimant housewife: Yashima Asaka

Now, a convenience store is about to transform into a hunting ground…
