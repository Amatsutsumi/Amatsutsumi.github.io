あらすじ#

One night, a student Ryo, tries out a astral projection and is sucked

into a world named MUGEN KAIROU (the corridor of dreams an fantasy).

Floating in the air, in an unknown world, he finally realizes his floating

in mid air is an impossibility and subsequently begins to fall.

A girl in a goth loli dress catches him but his preconception that it is

impossible to defy the laws of gravity is too strong and they both fall.

Just before smacking into the ground, levitation kicks in and they land safely.

“This is my Master? How unreliable—-” she mutters under her breath.

Ryo must fight to protect Nina from Klaus and return peace to this world.
