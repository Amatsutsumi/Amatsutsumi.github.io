あらすじ#

Yuki is attracted to his five older sisters but they’re getting older, looking for boyfriends, and talking about marriage. If he is to stop them he needs to become their lover himself.

‘Siblings can’t get married though,’ his older sister Rikka tells him. ‘But if you made them pregnant … they would be yours.’

Can Yuki put a baby in his favorite sisters?
