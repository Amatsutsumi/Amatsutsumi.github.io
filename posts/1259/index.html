あらすじ#

(Umm… what do you mean? In other words, this is a return to the past… a dream?)

I looked up at myself in the mirror.

No matter how you look at it, it was a boy, not an old man, but a boy, the old me.

This is the town where my family lived, where I lived for a while after I was born.

Moreover, old-fashioned houses.

Exactly back then…

“Icchan, welcome back”

……Grandma.

A real grandma, my beloved grandma who passed away a long time ago…

Grandma, who died a long time ago, is still alive!

Dreams, time slips, soulless near-death experiences, anything is fine.

I met my beloved grandmother.

That alone makes me very happy.

So anyway, I want to go with the flow here.

As long as it’s fun and happy, anything else…
