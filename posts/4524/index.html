あらすじ#

“The most talented girl in school, beautiful, excelling in both academics and sports, with a bright personality that everyone loves.”

That was the reputation of Matsuzaka Miyuki.

I considered myself lucky to be in the same class as her, the flawless honor student, right from the start of the school year.

And this bittersweet crush I had on her? That was what I thought true youth was all about.

When I suddenly decided to live alone at the start of spring, I thought my busy days would give my school life a boost, and everything would go smoothly from there.

But then… something happened.

One day, on my way home from school, I found a student handbook on the side of the road.

Curious, I opened it to see whose it was—and there was Miyuki’s photo and name.

Even more surprising, the address listed was near the apartment I’d just moved into.

Thinking this could be my chance to get closer to her, I decided to return the handbook to her house.

But as soon as I rang the doorbell, my naive expectations were shattered.

The Miyuki who appeared at the door looked nothing like the polished girl from school—she was sloppy, disheveled, and completely unlike her usual self.

I tried to convince myself that maybe she was just letting her guard down at home, but then I caught a glimpse of the pages in her handbook.

Inside were harsh comments and ratings about teachers and students—her true thoughts.

The flawless honor student… was a fake. A mask she wore.

As I stood there in shock, Miyuki pulled me inside and began explaining herself.

She said she’d wanted to create an ideal version of herself since she’d moved far away for school and didn’t know anyone.

She’d worked hard to maintain that image because she liked the way people looked at her.

Then, seeing how shaken I was, she begged me not to tell anyone her secret.

But the shock of learning that the girl I’d been crushing on was a fraud hit me harder than I expected.
